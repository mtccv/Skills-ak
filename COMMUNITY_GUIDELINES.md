## 参与规则 / Participation

只描述实际使用、可核查来源与明确的未知事项。区分亲自测试、来源转述和待核实判断；不虚构评分、收藏、兼容性或使用经历，不使用多账户操纵展示。

Describe actual use, verifiable sources and uncertainty. Distinguish tests you performed from quoted documentation or unverified assumptions. Do not fabricate reviews, favorites, compatibility or experience, or manipulate results through multiple accounts.

允许技术批评，不允许骚扰、人身攻击、冒充作者或公开无关个人资料。不要在评价、投稿、纠错或 Contact 中提交密码、API Key、Cookie、邮件验证链接、私人代码或无权披露的文件。

Technical criticism is welcome; harassment, impersonation and disclosure of unrelated personal information are not. Keep passwords, API keys, cookies, verification links, private code and unauthorized material out of submissions and reports.

## 评价、投稿与认领 / Reviews, submissions and claims

评价使用 1–5 整数评分，只能由本人基于真实体验提交。首次评价等待审核；已公开评价修改后创建新修订，待审或拒绝时旧批准版本继续公开计分，通过后原子切换。撤回或隐藏立即停止该评价的公开展示与评分贡献。管理员不能改写原文并冒充用户，也不能用 AI 生成用户评价。

Reviews require genuine experience and an integer rating from 1 to 5. First submissions require moderation. Edits create revisions; the approved revision remains public while the edit is pending or rejected. Approval switches the public revision atomically. Withdrawal or hiding removes its public contribution. Administrators and AI must not impersonate reviewers.

投稿填写仓库根 URL、独立 SKILL.md 相对路径及必要说明；同仓库不同路径可以是不同 Skills。使用本人已验证账户跟进草稿、待审、补充资料和撤回。历史匿名投稿不按邮箱认领。Accepted 仅表示接受进入后续流程，不直接发布或覆盖已审核资源、版本及译文。

Submissions identify a repository root and a separate SKILL.md path. Different paths may represent different Skills. Use your verified account to manage drafts, review requests, additional information and withdrawal. Historical anonymous messages are not claimed by matching email addresses. Acceptance starts further work; it does not publish or overwrite approved content.

Original Author、Repository Maintainer、Submitter 是不同身份。证据需具体可核查，不以仓库 owner 自动推定原创。认领、撤销和争议通过人工流程；批准只建立被核实的公开关联，不授予资源编辑或发布权限。公开开发者资料受本人公开偏好及审核控制，邮箱、登录名和私有证据不公开。

Original authors, repository maintainers and submitters have different roles. Provide specific evidence; repository ownership alone does not prove authorship. Approval establishes a verified public association, not editing or publishing permission. Public profiles follow user privacy choices and review gates; email addresses, login names and private evidence remain private.

## 举报、许可和安全 / Reports, licensing and safety

评价举报与 Skill 纠错是独立流程。请选择准确原因并提交必要、脱敏的证据；举报不自动下架。内部备注和举报人身份不公开。版权问题可通过 Contact 提供资源地址、具体内容和权利依据，不在公开评论中扩散个人材料。

Review reports and Skill corrections are separate workflows. Submit a specific reason with necessary sanitized evidence. Reports do not trigger automatic takedown. Reporter identities and internal notes remain private. Copyright inquiries should identify the resource, relevant material and evidence of rights through Contact.

收录、作者关系、链接、收藏和资源套装不代表平台拥有作品或获得商业再分发许可。使用、修改、分享及安装前核对原始 License 和附加条件。许可证未知时保持待核实，不把公开仓库等同任意使用授权。

Listing, attribution, links, favorites and bundles do not transfer ownership or grant commercial redistribution rights. Check original licenses and additional terms before use, modification, sharing or installation. An unknown license stays unverified; a public repository is not unrestricted permission.

SKILL.md、脚本、依赖和 AI 输出视为不可信资料。复制与分享只传递文字/地址；网站不执行安装命令。静态检查、AI 风险分析、人工审核和社区反馈都可能遗漏问题，Unknown/Low/Medium/High 不是无风险证明。风险报告不应附带真实密钥或要求管理员执行未知脚本。

Treat SKILL.md, scripts, dependencies and AI output as untrusted. Copy and share actions transfer text or URLs; they do not execute installation commands. Static checks, AI analysis, moderation and reviews may miss issues. Risk labels are not guarantees of safety. Do not include live credentials or ask administrators to execute unknown scripts.

## 隐私、邮件与处理反馈 / Privacy, mail and feedback

原生账户、收藏、未审核评价、投稿、通知和内部处理信息遵循既有私有边界。引导只保存少量已关闭主题标记。统计按各自明确用途取得同意，不能把注册、搜索、收藏或一种统计同意扩展为其他同意。

Accounts, favorites, unpublished reviews, submissions, notifications and internal records follow the existing privacy boundaries. Introductions save only bounded dismissed-topic preferences. Measurement uses purpose-specific consent; registration or agreement to one purpose does not authorize another.

精选订阅是独立主动选择与邮箱确认，不自动导入注册、Contact 或企业咨询邮箱。退订停止后续营销派发；必要账户安全邮件有独立用途。入队、传输受理和真实送达是不同状态。可通过账户隐私设置申请导出或删除；申请提交不表示全部信息立即清除，必要审计按实际匿名化与保留策略处理。

Digest subscriptions require separate opt-in and email confirmation. Registration, Contact and enterprise inquiries do not enroll users. Unsubscription stops subsequent marketing dispatch; account-security messages have a separate purpose. Queuing, transport acceptance and actual delivery are different states. Data export and erasure use verified account privacy requests; submitting a request does not mean immediate deletion of every record.

使用问题先查帮助；资源问题走 Skill 纠错，违规评价走举报；账户、隐私及其他问题使用 Contact。保存成功只说明私有记录已保存，不代表问题解决、订单成立或邮件送达。平台说明不得虚构响应时限、赔偿、认证、合同或法律保证。

Use Help for guidance, Skill corrections for resource problems, review reports for abusive reviews, and Contact for account, privacy or other inquiries. A saved message confirms a private record, not resolution, an order or email delivery. Published policies must not invent response-time, compensation, certification, contractual or legal guarantees.
